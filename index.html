Legacy Life Support

Legacy Life is a life simulator by Fidovasoft. For help, bug reports or questions about purchases, write to fidovasoft@gmail.com.

Common questions

I bought something on another phone. Open Settings, then Store, then Restore purchases, signed in with the same store account.

Where are my saves? Only on your device. Settings, then Backup, then Export saves them to a file you can keep or move to a new phone.

Can I play without ads? Yes: "Remove ads" or "Choose your life" in the Store turns ads off for good.
